Opening script

Read aloud before students begin.

For generations, the women of the Owens family have been witches. Their magic has been passed down from mother to daughter, but so has something much darker: a family curse.

Long ago, an Owens ancestor cast a spell after having her heart broken. Ever since, the men who fall in love with Owens women have been doomed to meet a tragic fate.

Sally and Gillian Owens grew up knowing about the curse. Sally eventually had two daughters of her own: Kylie and Antonia, better known as Toni. When her husband died, Sally erased all memories of magic from her two daughters, to protect them. But still, the curse remained. Growing up, Kylie and Toni were always told: Do not fall in love. It never ends well for an Owens woman.

You are Toni. And this is where our story begins.

A couple months ago, Kylie met Gideon. And despite all the warnings, she fell in love. When Gideon falls into a mysterious coma, Sally and Aunt Gilly come clean: They are witches. And the curse is real.

Determined to save him, Kylie does some research. She ends up finding the Book of Raven, a book of ancient magic. And then, she's gone. No warning. Just a note to you, her sister.

You're worried about Kylie, and you know she wouldn't have left without a good reason. You've entered her bedroom, hoping to figure out where she's gone and what she's discovered.

Everything you need may be hidden somewhere among her belongings.

Your mission: Uncover the secret of the Owens family curse, and finally, break the curse. If you can.

Start by reading Kylie's note to you on her desk.

And remember: in the Owens family, nothing is ever quite what it seems.

Hints, answers, and FAQs

Offer a hint first. Expand the answer only when a group needs more help.

What is the goal? Where should students start?

The goal is to break the Owens family curse. Start with Kylie's note, then read everything in the letters and on the desk. Encourage students to take notes and connect clues across rooms.

The family tree

Hint: The letters on the desk explain the family relationships. Use them to place the portraits on the family tree.

Show the answer

Top row, left to right: Maria — Vincent — Lily (Lillian).

Middle row, left to right: Regina — Daniel — Jet — Frances.

Bottom row: Gina — Kylie. These two can be placed in either order.

Unlocking the phone and contacting Gina

Hint: Look closely at the Polaroids and letters. Together, they provide the clues needed to unlock the phone and call Gina Owens.

After the call, Gina sends a text. Read the text thread carefully: it contains information needed to finish the potion puzzle.

Show the phone password

The password is 1031. Sally's birthday is Halloween, October 31.

Finding the right library book

Hint: Reread the letters for a reference to the book students should find in the library.

Show the answer

Find Alice in Wonderland in Aunt Franny's library. Open the book to find the riddle.

Completing the potion

Hint: Combine the library clue with Gina's text thread. The order matters, and the final ingredient is a birthstone.

Show the answer

Add the ingredients in this order:

  1. Sea salt
  2. Wormwood
  3. Sage
  4. Knotgrass
  5. Moonstone
  6. Lavender
  7. Rosemary
  8. July birthstone — Ruby

Then brew the potion.
