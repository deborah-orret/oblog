Black Cat Day

Hi! Happy Black Cat Day! 🐈‍⬛

Black Cat Days are celebrated at the end of each unit in middle school computer science. The goal of the day is just to take a break from coding, bond with classmates, do a hands on activity that's a little different than a traditional project, and pull the winning raffle tickets for each unit!

The following apps include logic puzzles, games, and class activities that have been designed and created for Black Cat Days. All of these activities have been designed and developed by Ms. Orret (me), but often inspired by other games. Feel free to reach out if you have an idea or notice a bug!

While AI was used in the software development of this website and many of these apps, AI was not used to ideate or design these games or to create any visual assets.
